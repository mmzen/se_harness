`REL-SEH-016` gates: **38 work orders, 21 verification contracts and 41 keyed evidence
paths**, over a union of **48 requirements**.

  `WO-REB-023-migration-scenario-successor.md` and `WO-REB-023-verification.md`. Zero gated
