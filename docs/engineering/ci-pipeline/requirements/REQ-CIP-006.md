# Requirement: Derive the predecessor evaluator facts from the declared governor

## Rationale

`0.6.0` is restated in five places and its wheel digest in two under
different names (`RELEASED_VERIFIER_WHEEL_SHA256`,
`PREDECESSOR_WHEEL_SHA256`). A version bump requires editing three
constants in `candidate-evidence.yml` and hand-writing a migration
fixture, or four jobs silently skip (`developing-se-harness.md`,
"Release sequences").

## Preconditions and trigger

Any job that installs or names the predecessor evaluator.

## Required response

- A first step derives `version`, `wheel_sha256` and `scenario` from
  `.engineering-harness.toml`, `.engineering-harness.lock` and
  `tests/fixtures/governance_migration/` and exports them as job outputs.
- Every later reference uses the outputs; no version literal or digest
  literal remains in the workflow.
- A missing scenario for the declared pair fails the job with the expected
  path in the message; nothing skips.
- The migration fixture is generated by the canonical writer from the same
  inputs, under a `harnessctl` or `repository_tools` command that the notes
  name.

## Failure and boundary behavior

The derivation step runs before any network access; a mismatch between the
lock's recorded digest and the downloaded wheel fails as today.

## Constraints

The managed `engineering-harness.yml` keeps its version literal until the
governor upgrade moves it; this requirement binds the repository-owned
workflows.

## Acceptance examples

**Given** `pyproject.toml` raised with no new scenario

**Then** the derivation step fails naming the missing scenario path, and no
job is skipped.
