# Work Order: Derive the predecessor evaluator facts from the declared governor

## Lifecycle

Approval authorizes only the scope below. Start, completion, commit-bound
verification and any release decision are separate accountable acts.
Starts after `WO-CIP-001` is implemented, on the same workflows.

## Objective

P6: one derivation step exporting `version`, `wheel_sha256`, `scenario`;
no literal left in the repository-owned workflows; a scenario writer
command; failure closed when the scenario is missing.

## In scope

`CIP-PRE` 1–3; `CIP-DOC`: `developing-se-harness.md` "Release sequences"
(the version-bump step rewritten to name the command), "Advancing the root
evaluator"; `harnessctl-reference.md` for the writer; `ci-pipeline.md`.

## Out of scope

The managed `engineering-harness.yml` literal; the lock format; the
migration scenario's content.

## Authorized decision envelope

Whether the writer is a `harnessctl` subcommand or a `repository_tools`
module; the notes name whichever it is.

## Constraints

The derivation runs before any network step; the lock is read, never
written.

## Expected change surface

Two workflows, one module, one fixture regenerated by the writer, notes,
tests, evidence.

## Required verification

`VER-CIP-001` row 6 and scenario 5; repository-required checks; full suite;
handoff check.

## Evidence to record

Under `docs/engineering/ci-pipeline/evidence/WO-CIP-003/`: the grep, the
failure-injection run, the regenerated fixture's diff (expected empty).

## Stop and escalate conditions

Stop if the lock does not carry the digest the derivation needs, or if the
regenerated fixture differs from the committed one.

## Completion report format

The `harnessctl check . --artifact WO-CIP-003 --checkpoint handoff` schema-2
block verbatim with the complete changed-path set, and its `result_sha256`.
