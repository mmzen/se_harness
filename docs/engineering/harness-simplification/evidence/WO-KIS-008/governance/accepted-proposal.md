# Accepted shared rule and integration

Choose the least complex design that satisfies the agreed requirements and constraints. Justify material added complexity by the need it serves and why a simpler approach is insufficient. Consider the effort to understand, build, test, operate and change the whole solution. Defer speculative needs. Review both the necessity of the specified behavior and the simplicity of its implementation. Preserve correctness and the project's required quality and safety properties.

The sole maintained product wording and questions belong in ARTIFACT_AUTHORING.md.
Existing type checklists cover needs, necessary promises, simpler alternatives and
proportionate verification. A final section covers review of implemented changes.
Templates and the shared change/evidence skill routes link to the policy. The router
requires it for authoring, design and review; ordinary start/review manifests include it.
Use the existing checklist reader, normal decisions and evidence. Introduce no new
artifact type, score, receipt, lifecycle state, machine simplicity predicate or CI job.
Current root copies are adopted only through a later ordinary release/upgrade action.
